# About the Author

## Alaina Scott

Alaina Scott is a computer scientist and the creator of **SCTT** (Smooth Cubical Type Theory) and **Lemma**, a geometric construction environment for accessible formal verification. Her work focuses on making the machinery of dependent type theory tangible — replacing symbolic proof scripts with spatial composition, and extending cubical type theory with smooth/differentiable structure drawn from differential geometry.

Her research interests span the intersection of type theory, constructive mathematics, formal verification, and human-centered design. Lemma emerged from a conviction that the accessibility barriers in existing formal verification tools are architectural problems, not inevitable tradeoffs — that type checking reduces to edge compatibility checking, and that proofs can be assembled from pre-verified tiles rather than written as text.

SCTT extends Cartesian cubical type theory (following ABCFHL) with a smooth layer: paths are C∞ by default, Kan operations preserve differentiability order, and types can carry smooth manifold structure. The theory bridges homotopy type theory with synthetic differential geometry, enabling calculus and analysis to be performed within the type-theoretic foundation itself.

She can be reached through the SCTT project repository at [github.com/tensorhusker/SCTT](https://github.com/tensorhusker/SCTT).
